STUDENT PROTOTYPE / Social impact

Volunteer Connect

Govardhan Eco Village reference project

A low-code/no-code portal prototype for volunteer participation in sustainability and community initiatives.

Process mappingService designLow-code/no-code prototypingEngagement dashboards
THE CHALLENGE

Turn intent into participation

Create a scalable way to manage growing volunteer participation, helping people explore ESG projects, register and track their contributions.

PEOPLE IN THE PICTURE
  • Volunteers and alumni volunteers
  • Partner organisations
  • Internal teams
  • Local communities
  • Government and advocacy groups
  • Donors and supporters
THE THINKING BEHIND THE IDEA

From understanding
to opportunity.

  1. 01

    The stakeholder map links volunteer engagement with partner needs, CSR needs and digital transformation.

  2. 02

    Registration needs to capture availability, interests, experience and languages so participation can be coordinated.

  3. 03

    The proposed portal brings registration, application status, departments, feedback and a dashboard into one experience.

THE PROTOTYPE

A closer look.

The student low-code/no-code prototype explores volunteer registration, engagement tracking and automated coordination for Govardhan Eco Village. The design proposes clearer workflows and aggregate activity views; it does not establish measured operational improvements.
