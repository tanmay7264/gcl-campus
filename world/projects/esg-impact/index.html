STUDENT PROTOTYPE / Sustainability

ESG Impact Dashboard

Team Sustain X · retail-banking study

A retail-banking prototype that brings personal ESG insights, spending analytics and sustainability education together.

Consumer researchESG opportunity mappingDashboard designPrototype evaluation
THE CHALLENGE

Make sustainability visible

Assess ESG practices in Indian retail banking, understand consumer expectations and identify opportunities to integrate ESG into banking products.

PEOPLE IN THE PICTURE
  • Retail banking customers
  • Banking product teams
  • Sustainability teams
THE THINKING BEHIND THE IDEA

From understanding
to opportunity.

  1. 01

    The reference explores literature, competitive analysis and consumer surveys to frame the opportunity.

  2. 02

    Transparent ESG scoring and granular analytics can help explain the relationship between banking and sustainability.

  3. 03

    Guided onboarding, clear visuals, security and privacy are identified as areas for refinement.

THE PROTOTYPE

A closer look.

The student impact-dashboard prototype presents ESG scoring and spending analytics within a mobile banking experience. The concept also explores sustainability education and incentives; these are proposed features, with no verified adoption or impact results.
