LEADERSHIP BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

Real challenges.
Wider perspectives.

GCL combines classroom learning with live immersion in a real issue or need.

The aim is to develop self-aware, socially aware and agile leaders who can empathise across perspectives, collaborate courageously and create positive change.
