Global Citizen Leaders (GCL)

Share a real challenge from your organisation. Our Post Graduate management teams spend two trimesters turning it into a researched, prototyped, commercially feasible solution, mentored by WeSchool faculty and by you.

About the programModelled on "Leadership Beyond Boundaries" by the USA-based Center for Creative Leadership (CCL) and Design Impact (DI). Classroom learning combined with hands-on immersion in a live, real-world need.
